# Patent-family note

## Primary family represented in the package

- **NL 240201** - Netherlands priority application, filed 15 June 1959.
- **GB 915,767 A** - English family publication, published 16 January 1963: *Improvements in or relating to the production of thermoplastic products derived from di-epoxy compounds*.
- **DE 1,190,672 B** - German family publication, published 8 April 1965.
- **SE 301 717 B** - Swedish publication supplied by the Klootwijk family, published 17 June 1968.
- **US 3,364,178 A** - corresponding U.S. product-focused disclosure, published 16 January 1968: *Thermoplastic poly(hydroxyalkyl diphenyl sulfone) ethers*.

The bundled U.S. document is particularly useful because its product wording and comparison table make the sulfone structure-property argument more explicit. It names Robert W. F. Kreps, Arie Klootwijk and Johan M. Goppel as inventors.

The GB and DE publications were used as cross-check sources but are not bundled as PDFs. Their public records can be consulted at:

- https://patents.google.com/patent/GB915767A/en
- https://patents.google.com/patent/DE1190672B/en
- https://patents.google.com/patent/NL240201A/en

The patent-family relationships and dates are also tabulated in `../04_Data/patent_family.csv`.
