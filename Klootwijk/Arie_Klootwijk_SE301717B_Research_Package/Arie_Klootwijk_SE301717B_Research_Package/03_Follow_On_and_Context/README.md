# Follow-on and contextual patents

These documents are evidence of later technological context, analogous material behaviour or plausible lineage. Their inclusion does not prove that the exact 1959 polymer became a particular later commercial product.

## EP 0 249 262 A2 / A3

*Thermoplastic polymer composition having thermosetting processing characteristics.* This later Shell disclosure is relevant because it treats linear diphenol-diepoxide-derived chains as processable thermoplastics and then uses their secondary hydroxyls for controlled crosslinking. It supports the interpretation of the Klootwijk chemistry as a latent reactive-thermoplastic platform.

## US 6,451,878 B1 and WO 2001/070843 A2

*High molecular weight epoxy resin and resinous composition for printed circuit board.* These documents provide later electronics context for BPS-containing high-molecular-weight epoxy/phenoxy materials, with close attention to molecular weight, equivalent ratio, purity and film performance. They support PCB dielectric/adhesive film as a strong modern application reading, without establishing continuous product lineage.

## US 5,076,935 A

*Filtration membranes made from polyethersulfone/phenoxy resin blend.* This document supports the general plausibility of using hydroxyether/phenoxy chemistry in PES-based membrane blends. It does not establish that the exact all-sulfone Klootwijk polymer is a validated membrane material.

## US 4,108,837 A

*Polyarylene polyethers.* This is included primarily as a structural and process contrast. Commercial PSU/PESU families are fully aromatic polyarylene ethers commonly formed by nucleophilic aromatic substitution. They are distinct from the hydroxypropyl-containing Klootwijk poly(hydroxy ether), even though both use sulfone-containing aromatic building blocks and both are sensitive to stoichiometry, water and purity.

## US 4,657,954 A

*Poly(hydroxy ether).* This later patent documents the established application range of linear phenoxy/poly(hydroxy ether) materials - coatings, laminates, adhesives, films, fibres, moulding materials, resin modifiers and anticorrosive binders - and their ability to crosslink through abundant hydroxyl groups. It also repeats a 0.98-1.02 substantially equimolar window, reinforcing the ordinary step-growth interpretation of the Klootwijk ratio language.
