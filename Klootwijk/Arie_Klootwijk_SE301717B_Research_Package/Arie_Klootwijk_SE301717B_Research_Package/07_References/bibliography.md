## Notes on evidence use

- Patent documents are used as primary historical and technical sources. Their example data are not treated as independently replicated scientific measurements.
- Later patents support technological plausibility and lineage but do not establish that the exact 1959 polymer was commercialized in the later application.
- The 2004 paper confirms the continued material identity “poly(hydroxyether sulfone)” and hydrogen-bonding behavior; its synthetic route is not identical to every claim of SE 301 717 B.
- Current BPS hazard listings concern the low-molecular-weight chemical. They do not by themselves establish the hazard of a purified high-molecular-weight polymer, but they make residual monomer, oligomer and degradation-product analysis essential.
