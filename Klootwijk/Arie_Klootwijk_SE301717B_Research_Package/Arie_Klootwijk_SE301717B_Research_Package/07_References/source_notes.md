## Calculations

- Historical yield stress was converted using `1 kgf/cm2 = 0.0980665 MPa`.
- The Carothers-Flory imbalance equation used is `Xn = (1+r)/(1+r-2rp)`, with `r` defined as the smaller functional-group population divided by the larger.
- At full limiting-group conversion, `Xn,max = (1+r)/(1-r)`.
- Example 1 effective dihydroxy feed is interpreted as `0.97 mol neutral BPS + 0.05 mol monosodium BPS = 1.02 mol`, because the patent expressly says phenol present as salt is included in the ratio.
- Example 4's 2.5 g BPS stopping charge is approximately 0.010 mol using a BPS molar mass near 250.27 g/mol.
- Example 4 intrinsic-viscosity change from 1.37 to 1.29 is approximately a 5.8% decrease; this is not converted into molecular-weight loss because the exponent and calibration are unknown.

## Terminology

- `BPS`: bisphenol S, 4,4'-sulfonyldiphenol, historically p,p'-diphenylol sulfone.
- `DGE-BPS`: diglycidyl ether of bisphenol S.
- `DGEBA`: diglycidyl ether of bisphenol A.
- `poly(hydroxy ether)`: linear polymer formed by repeated phenol/epoxide ring-opening, with a secondary hydroxyl generated at each event; often associated with phenoxy-resin terminology.
- `PSU/PESU`: commercial fully aromatic polysulfone/polyethersulfone families typically prepared by nucleophilic aromatic substitution. These are structurally distinct from the Klootwijk poly(hydroxy ether).
