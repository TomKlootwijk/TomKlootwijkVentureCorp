THE LITTLE-KID VERSION

A box of mixed marbles

Imagine your red marble goes into a box with 199 marbles from other people. The box then pours marbles into two cups. We know exactly which cups were filled and how many marbles went into each cup. We do not know which cup got your exact red marble. The map colors each cup by its fair share. That color is a careful estimate.
