# Auditable absence governance checklist

- [ ] A legitimate expectation or obligation exists.
- [ ] The expectation has a versioned authority and scope.
- [ ] The relevant person, object, service or process identity is resolved.
- [ ] Observation support and coverage were valid for the declared interval.
- [ ] Time synchronization and channel health were verified.
- [ ] The compatibility predicate was evaluated under the correct consent, policy, mode and branch.
- [ ] The due interval closed.
- [ ] No matching compatible event exists.
- [ ] Opt-out, transfer, maintenance, recipient unavailability and other valid exceptions were checked.
- [ ] Incomplete coverage produces `UNKNOWN`, not `ABSENT`.
- [ ] Reason codes, confidence, predicate version and provenance are retained.
- [ ] Human correction, appeal and amendment are available where the event affects people or public services.
