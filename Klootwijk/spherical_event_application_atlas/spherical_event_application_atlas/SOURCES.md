# Evidence registry

The complete machine-readable registry is in `data/evidence_registry.csv` and `data/evidence_registry.json`.

- **C0** - uploaded Topological Cross-Domain Corpus Report: the query-first operator vocabulary and bounded feasibility discipline.
- **R1-R5** - MRI baseline, quantitative/sparse MRI precedent and MR safety.
- **R6-R11** - FHIR, digital-health governance and mental-health research framing.
- **R12-R14** - ASML computational lithography, EUV and High-NA context.
- **R15-R20** - grid, PV, pipeline monitoring and rupture-response context.
- **R21-R25, R35** - smart-city, municipal service, sensing, KPI and privacy context.
- **R26-R27** - supply-chain event and traceability standards.
- **R28-R29** - provenance and FAIR research-data principles.
- **R30-R34** - event sensing, level-crossing ADC, photonic computing and continuous collision detection.

External sources establish component feasibility, standards or baseline practice. They do not validate the integrated application atlas.
