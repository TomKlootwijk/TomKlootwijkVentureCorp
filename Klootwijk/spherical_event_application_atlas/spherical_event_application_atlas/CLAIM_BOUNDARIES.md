# Claim boundaries

## Retained from the source corpus

- directly queryable state and event semantics;
- local spherical/radial-angular support;
- explicit relation or guard surfaces;
- compatibility-gated coupling;
- typed transitions and narrow parity/routing flags;
- identity by lineage and invariants rather than current coordinates;
- finite grammar plus exogenous novelty log;
- images, grids and documents as downstream views;
- bounded experiments and matched baselines.

## External anchors used

Official standards, regulators, national laboratories, standards bodies and primary papers are used to establish component classes and current baselines: MRI and safety, FHIR, WHO health-AI governance, RDoC, ASML lithography, NIST smart grids/cities, NREL PV/digital twins, PHMSA pipeline monitoring, GS1 traceability, W3C provenance, FAIR data, event sensors, level-crossing ADCs, photonic circuits and continuous collision detection.

## Cross-domain inferences

Examples include adaptive MRI acquisition, task-first reconstruction, a cradle-to-life health event graph, ASML-adjacent hotspot and lineage systems, verified pipeline mitigation events, PV event twins, event-native ADC pipelines, auditable municipal service absence and claim-evidence document graphs.

## Not established

- a universal MRI replacement;
- the obsolescence of ASML/EUV lithography or advanced semiconductor nodes;
- the elimination of matrices, ADCs, PDE solvers, rasterization or ray tracing in every workload;
- zero heat, power, latency, memory or calibration;
- exact prediction of arbitrary biology, weather, markets, people or chaotic systems;
- autonomous diagnosis, therapy, mental-health inference or gene control;
- literal physical shielding, invisibility, containment or immunity from topological metaphors;
- automatic fossil-fuel reduction without measured operational or infrastructure change.
