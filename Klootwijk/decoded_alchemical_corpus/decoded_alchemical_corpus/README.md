# Decoded Alchemical Corpus

This package converts the uploaded documents into a technical knowledge archive while preserving the difference between:

- source-defined operators;
- established mathematics/physics/chemistry/biology;
- bounded engineering proposals;
- decoder inferences;
- unsupported, incorrect, fictional or purely metaphorical claims.

## Start here

1. `Corpus_Decoder_Report.pdf` - illustrated human-readable report.
2. `01_master_synthesis.md` - compact technical through-line.
3. `data/alchemical_to_technical_lexicon.csv` - 70-term decoder.
4. `data/equations_and_operators.csv` - explicit formulas and requirements.
5. `data/claims_evidence_matrix.csv` - 51 claims with verdicts and tests.
6. `structures/` - chemical, geometric, biological and photonic assets.
7. `per_document/` - source-by-source analyses.
8. `source_text/` - full text extracted page by page from the supplied PDFs.

## Important conclusion

The corpus does contain a coherent computational architecture: a finite grammar over a phase-layered state manifold with local spherical support, compatibility gating, event solving, transition routing, lineage and an exogenous novelty log.

The most practical hardware interpretation is a bounded optofluidic waveguide demonstrator (Hollowlens-0).

The corpus does **not** contain a uniquely recoverable hidden chemistry for all its metaphors. Molecular files are supplied only for unambiguously identified chemicals:

- N,N-dimethyltryptamine (DMT);
- tryptamine and indole as decoder-added structural scaffolds;
- water and glycerol as explicitly named optofluidic materials;
- dimethylamine as a decoder-added DMT fragment.

Proteins, genes, viruses, blood plasma and liquid crystals are not unique molecules; they receive schematics and constraints rather than invented atomistic structures.

## Asset types

### Chemistry

Each molecule may include:

- SVG 2D structure;
- MOL and SDF connectivity;
- PDB generated 3D conformer;
- canonical SMILES;
- JSON metadata.

Generated conformers are illustrative computed geometries, not experimentally determined structures.

## Evidence boundary

The later source documents explicitly reject zero latency, zero heat, zero power, zero memory, universal O(1), literal double vacuum, physical Klein-bottle proof, general AI compression, medical effects and replacement of all renderers/PDE solvers.

BCI, telepresence and bodily-control passages in ADDominUS are catalogued as unsupported narrative. They are not treated as evidence of remote bodily occupation and not converted into a medical mechanism.

## Rebuilding
