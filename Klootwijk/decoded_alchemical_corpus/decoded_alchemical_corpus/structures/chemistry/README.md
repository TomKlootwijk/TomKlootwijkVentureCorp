# Chemistry Assets

## DMT

`dmt.svg`, `.mol`, `.sdf`, `.pdb`, `.smiles.txt`, `.metadata.json`

Standard neutral N,N-dimethyltryptamine, formula C12H16N2. The 3D PDB is a generated UFF-optimized conformer and is not an experimentally determined biological structure.

## Scaffolds and fragments

- `indole.*` - fused aromatic heterocycle.
- `tryptamine.*` - indole-3-ethylamine scaffold.
- `dimethylamine.*` - fragment used to explain the terminal amine; in DMT the nitrogen is tertiary after attachment to the side chain.

## Optofluidic materials

- `water.*`
- `glycerol.*`

These are included because the bounded hardware source explicitly lists water/glycerol mixtures. Exact mixtures must be characterized experimentally.

## Evidence status

DMT, water and glycerol are standard-science annotations to named chemicals. Indole, tryptamine and dimethylamine are decoder-added explanatory substructures. No claim is made that the source prose secretly encoded their atom coordinates.
