WORKING DEFINITION
Practical technical waveguide liquid-substrate lensing is an optofluidic photonic front end in
which a local radial-angular field is focused or phase-shaped by a controllable liquid region,
coupled into selected guided modes, filtered by wavelength/polarization/mode/phase
compatibility, and converted into a verified event only when a measured guard condition is
crossed.

WHAT "SPHERICAL" MEANS
A local chart around the coupler:
angular support, radial reach,
orientation, uncertainty, or field of view.
It does not require a global spherical
world model.
WHAT "LIQUID-SUBSTRATE" MEANS
A liquid region is bonded to, embedded
in, or optically coupled to a solid
photonic substrate. It tunes curvature,
effective index, phase, or coupling.
WHAT "THROUGHPUT" MEANS
Verified events or answered queries
per second at a declared error budget.
It is not merely photon flux, baud rate,
or propagation velocity.
PRIMARY DEFINITION OF SPHERICAL THROUGHPUT
Count only outputs that were inside the declared spherical support, compatible with the selected channel, crossed the specified guard, and met the
confidence or certification threshold.

waveguide and liquid-lens hardware.

GOVERNING PHYSICS
The equations that replace metaphor
Optics, fluid interface, guided modes, and event semantics
Practical waveguide liquid-substrate lensing | ADDominUS corpus integration | July 2026
5
1. OPTICAL FIELD
Solve the electromagnetic field in the actual dielectric geometry.
The actuator state u changes the permittivity through the liquid
shape or index.
2. GUIDED-MODE EVOLUTION
Coupled-mode theory tracks attenuation, propagation constants,
and tunable coupling between modes. This is the practical
"matrix-in-glass".
3. LIQUID INTERFACE + PHASE
Young-Laplace links pressure to meniscus curvature. The
effective-index change creates the programmable optical phase.
4. TRANSIENT ACTUATION
Use Navier-Stokes only when the lens shape is moving quickly
enough that quasi-static curvature is insufficient. Light itself
remains governed by Maxwell.
5. SPHERICAL-TO-MODE COUPLING
The overlap integral measures how much of the admitted
radial-angular field couples into the chosen guided mode.
6. BOUNDED COMPATIBILITY EVENT
A transition exists only when the input lies in support, the channel
is compatible, and the measured guard reaches zero.
TOPOLOGY DISCIPLINE
A "Klein bottle" can be retained as a routing or gluing abstraction only after its ports, orientation map, and
transfer matrix are specified. It is not a substitute for Maxwell, coupled-mode theory, or fluid mechanics.
Equations are the bounded engineering formalization of the corpus. They are not extracted verbatim from ADDominUS, whose displayed formulas are incomplete in the source
export.

COMPATIBILITY PREDICATE
Compatibility can be partly physical (wavelength, polarization, mode, phase) and partly digital (time window, permission, provenance).
Co-location at the coupler is not enough.
EVENT STATE MACHINE
IDLE
ADMIT SUPPORT
TUNE LIQUID
MATCH MODE
CROSS GUARD
LATCH B.C.E.
LOG / RESET
ONE-BIT PARITY - NARROW ROLE
p = 0
Not admitted, route A, stale, unavailable, or incompatible -
according to the declared convention.
p = 1
Admitted, route B, fresh, available, or compatible. The bit
must never be interpreted without its schema.
A B.C.E. may toggle p, but the optical amplitudes,
thresholds, uncertainty, and lineage remain separate
state.
REFERENCE CONTROL LOOP
support = admit(theta, phi, r)
lens_u  = tune(target_mode)
y       = detector_read()
chi     = mode_ok AND phase_ok
          AND time_ok AND policy_ok
if support AND chi AND guard(y):
    parity = NOT parity
    emit(event, confidence, lineage)
else:
    reject_or_hold()
WHY A DIGITAL SIDECAR REMAINS
Stores calibration coefficients and liquid actuator state.
Applies thresholds, uncertainty, lineage, and policy tags.
Detects drift, bubbles, temperature shifts, and detector saturation.
Provides a conventional reference path and safe fallback.
B.C.E. retains the earlier Bounded Compatibility Event mnemonic while grounding it in measured optical and control variables.

CLAIMS THIS INTEGRATION DOES NOT MAKE
No zero latency: propagation is fast, but coupling, liquid actuation, detection, conversion, and control take time.
No zero heat or zero power: lasers, modulators, actuators, detectors, and electronics dissipate energy.
No universal O(1), zero-memory universe, exact chaos, or topological immunity from missed events.
No literal double vacuum or physical Klein-bottle proof unless a fabricable geometry and transfer map are specified.
No general AI compression, medical effect, biological mechanism, or replacement for all renderers and PDE solvers.
This bounded interpretation follows the corpus rule: retain useful operators, require a digital reference, and treat hardware claims as frontier until measured.

NEW CANONICAL CORPUS ADDITION
The spherical throughput of practical technical waveguide liquid-substrate lensing is the
verified event or query rate produced when a bounded spherical input support is coupled
through a tunable liquid optical interface into compatible guided modes, with transitions
declared only at measured guard crossings and with optical loss, error, energy, actuator
latency, calibration, uncertainty, and lineage included in the result.
Canonical shorthand: local support -> liquid-controlled coupling -> compatible mode -> B.C.E. -> verified output.
