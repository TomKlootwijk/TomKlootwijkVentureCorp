# S3 - DMT / Leeuwenhoek / Optical-Perceptual Corpus

## Role

Optical and sensory origin narrative. The useful technical layer concerns apertures, local polar organization, inversion, sensor sieves, jitter, reflection, and device-mediated scale. Later political, psychological, and biological literalizations are not treated as system proof.

## Technical contributions by page

- **pp. 1-3:** lens as spatial/frequency filter; spectral density, radial/angular harmonics, logarithmic compression, and stacked polar LUTs.
- **pp. 5-10:** camera-obscura pinhole, inversion, Möbius continuity, inward/outward reflection, and the focal-point metaphor.
- **pp. 11-15:** external devices establish numerical scale; biological receptor mosaics are sieves; micro-jitter/pixel shift fills sampling gaps.
- **pp. 16-20:** spherical LUT, kinematic calculus, procedural grammar, the frontend CCD problem, and the explicit rejection of cubes/raymarching as the core.
- **pp. 24-30:** sensor diversity across eyes, whiskers, sonar, and UV; local interfaces differ while a shared spatial problem remains.
- **pp. 30-33:** an important self-correction: without a defined zero/event surface, continuous-field language can hide sampling and rasterization.
- **later pages:** increasingly political, personal, and metaphorical narratives. These remain provenance only and are excluded from the technical core.

## Normalization used here

- aperture/sieve -> local support and sensor transfer function;
- jitter -> controlled sampling/dither operator;
- camera obscura/Möbius -> inversion/orientation motifs;
- spherical LUT -> local radial-angular address space, not a universal atom model.
