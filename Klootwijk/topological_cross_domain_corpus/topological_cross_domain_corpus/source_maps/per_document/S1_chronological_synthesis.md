Primary interpretive authority for this package. It changes the reading rule from renderer-first to ontology/query-first.

- **p. 1:** states the decisive shift: rasterization, raymarching, voxel storage, and frame loops are downstream projections; the core is queryable relations, events, phases, and identities. Defines local spherical support, lineage identity, and the bounded feasibility verdict.
- **pp. 2-5:** reconstructs the corpus chronology and shows the direction of correction from shader/sensor metaphors toward a declarative state-and-event calculus.
- **pp. 5-7:** provides the unified concept, product state space, entity/world equations, relation/support/compatibility event definition, and the double-vacuum interpretation.
- **pp. 7-9:** defines Equation World Zero, bounded complexity claims, numerical risks, and the headless versus hybrid implementation paths.
- **pp. 9-10:** separates rasterization, raymarching, tracing, and the proposed substrate by the questions each answers.
- **pp. 11-12:** names the real bottlenecks and gives six prototype queries and exit criteria.
