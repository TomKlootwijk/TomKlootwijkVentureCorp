# S5 - ADDominUS

## Role

Cross-domain consolidation that explicitly opens with a queryable relation algebra and state/event calculus. It extends the architecture into identity, memory, industry, biology, and photonics, but mixes disciplined operators with totalizing and personal metaphors.

## Technical contributions by page

- **pp. 1-2:** relation algebra, state/event calculus, local spherical supports, optional visual projections, phase alignment, and lighthouse incidence.
- **pp. 6-11:** lineage identity, ontological address, `Person(t) != Snapshot(t)`, finite grammar, exogenous log, branch history, and novelty-proportional memory.
- **pp. 13-16:** double vacuum, phase sheets, compatibility, bit masks, and replacement of universal frame loops with state/event queries.
- **pp. 32-33:** clearest internal explanation of double vacuum: same position, different sector; compatibility gate; one-bit parity; pinch-point routing.
- **pp. 41-45:** industrial twins, analytic guards, lineage/provenance, and parity/transition prompts.
- **pp. 47-50, 58-64:** etched glass/silicon photonics, matrix-in-glass, liquid lensing, waveguides, and manufacturing prompts later bounded by S6.
- **p. 54:** B=0 boundary, pinch point, parity route, invariant axis.

## Exclusions

Claims of physical untouchability, retroactive bodily control, general medical intervention, universal AI compression, zero heat, zero memory, and guaranteed O(1) are not carried into the technical core.
